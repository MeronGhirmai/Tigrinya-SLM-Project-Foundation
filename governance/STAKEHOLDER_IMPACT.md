# Module 5: Contextual Impact Statement & Stakeholder Analysis

## 1. Socio-Technical Impact Statement
Over 9 million Tigrinya speakers face systemic exclusion in mainstream LLMs due to Ge'ez tokenization inflation and corpus skew. However, deploying an SLM is not merely a mathematical optimization—it reshapes epistemic trust and linguistic authority. By optimizing `google/gemma-2-2b` via 4-bit QLoRA (~7.61 GB VRAM), this project democratizes educational Q&A, public health summarization, and bilingual translation on single-GPU hardware while enforcing guardrails against automation bias and dialectal erasure.

## 2. Participatory Stakeholder Matrix & Dialectal Pluralism
- **Native Speakers Across Borders & Dialects:** Explicitly respects both Eritrean (Asmara/Central) and Ethiopian (Mekelle/Tigrayan, Adwa, Raya, Shire) lexical and morphological varieties.
- **Tigrinya Linguists & Educators:** Co-designers of our 4-Dimension Human Evaluation Rubric (`outputs/human_eval_sheet.csv`) auditing SOV agreement, Fidel orthography, dialectal respect, and factual safety.
